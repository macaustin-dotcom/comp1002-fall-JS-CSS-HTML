    Debugging

    Errors are clues

    When something breaks, follow these steps:

    1. Open the console with F12.
    2. Read the red message: the kind of error, what went wrong, and the line number.
    3. Fix that line, save, and refresh.

    Try the break-it experiments at the bottom of js/script.js.

    Lab: practice exercises

    Write your answers in js/lab.js. Results show up below.

    1. Greeting: store your name and age in variables, then print a sentence using a template literal.
    2. Counting: use a for loop to print 1 to 10, then a while loop to count down from 10 and print "Blast off!"
    3. Even or odd: write a function that takes a number and returns "even" or "odd".
